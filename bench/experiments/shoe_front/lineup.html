Which shoe is on the figure?

Ten real pairs, drawn on the figure's feet three ways and shuffled. For each picture, tap the shoe you think it is. There are 30 pictures; it takes about four minutes.

The feet are shown at the size they are on your phone. Use the app's size first; the larger view is there if you want a second look.

You are not told which way each picture was drawn until the end.
